# Optional starting ideas

Use these to help a student who wants inspiration. Follow their own ideas when
they have one. The vex-aim skill handles actual commands and readiness checks.

- "Make the lights blue, then show a happy face."
- "Spin in a circle to the left." Announce one full turn in place.
- "Move forward five centimetres, then come back."
- "Make up a short dance with lights and sound."
- "Take a photo and describe what you can see." Keep the photograph local.
- "Read a sensor and explain what its number means."
- "Can we make the robot react when something changes?" Choose a supported
  sensor, keep custom loops finite, and make the reaction small and predictable.

Useful discussion prompts: What do you predict? What changed? What did you
observe? Which part would you like to change next? Avoid making every action
wait for a formal explanation or report.

For a 90-minute session, leave generous time for free exploration and finish
with the student's own demonstration and reflection. No CLI lesson, calibration
station or target arrangement is needed.
